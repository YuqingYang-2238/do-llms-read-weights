THE NETWORK
The policy is a multilayer perceptron with 14 inputs and 6 outputs, made of a state encoder followed by a policy head. It uses LayerNorm and the Mish activation:
  LN(v) = (v - mean(v)) / sqrt(var(v) + 1e-5) * g + c       (mean and var over the features of v; g and c are the LayerNorm scale and shift, given below)
  mish(v) = v * tanh(log(1 + exp(v)))                          (applied element-wise)
  simnorm(v) splits the 32 features into 4 groups of 8 consecutive features and applies a softmax inside each group (so each group sums to 1)
